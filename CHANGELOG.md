## 4.0.0 - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Added a first-use tutorial explaining the phrase hierarchy and addon workflow,
  with a Tutorial button in Settings to reopen it.
- Added personality, category, race, and class selectors to phrase management.

### Changed

- Reorganized the manager into Phrases and Settings tabs, moving personality
  and category management into Settings alongside profiles and language.
- Refreshed the main and manager panels with an opaque, Leatrix Plus-inspired
  style and made Personality the first main-panel selector.
- Expanded phrase selection to favor class-specific phrases while retaining
  race-wide phrases for variety and when class phrases are unavailable.
